Typing Bangla on a keyboard is slow for many people. Voice typing lets you say a sentence and see it appear as text. This guide shows how to do it on Windows 10 or 11 with **SHUNCHi**, a free app that types Bangla and English wherever your cursor is.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit)
- A microphone, built in or external
- An internet connection
- A free Gemini API key from Google (the next article shows how to get one)

## Step 1: Install SHUNCHi

Go to the [download page](/download/) and run the installer. No administrator rights are needed. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The app is new and not yet widely downloaded, which is why Windows asks.

## Step 2: Add your free key

Open SHUNCHi, go to **Settings**, paste your Gemini API key and choose **Save & test**. A green **Connected** label means you are ready. Your key is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC.

## Step 3: Speak and see the text appear

1. Click where you want to type: a browser, WhatsApp, Word, Notepad or any other app.
2. Press **Win + Left Ctrl** to speak Bangla, or **Win + Left Shift** to speak English.
3. Say what you want to write, then stop talking.
4. After a short pause, the finished text is typed at your cursor.

You can press the same shortcut again to finish early.

## Tips for better results

- Speak at a natural pace, in full sentences.
- Pick a quiet room. SHUNCHi learns the sound of your room, so a fan or an AC does not confuse it.
- If you speak slowly or pause a lot, choose **Relaxed** under "Finish after a pause" in Settings.
- You can mix English words into Bangla speech. They are written correctly too.

## A note on privacy

Your audio is sent to Google only to be turned into text, using your own key. On Google's free tier, content may be used to improve Google's products, so avoid dictating secrets. Your history stays on your PC.
